## Product description

COPILOTO VECTOR, developed by Patagonia Wings, brings flight preparation, operational context and post-flight analysis together in a Windows desktop application. Its own development includes flight planning assistance, live telemetry, multi-monitor displays through STREAMER BAR and LIVE PANEL, GSX ground-service synchronization, local ACARS-style flight logging, career progression and flight debriefing. The public website includes authentic application screenshots, with QA demonstrations distinguished from real simulator validation.

## Planned SayIntentions integration

We would like to integrate SayIntentions SAPI and communication history to display persistent ATC and pilot transcripts below STREAMER BAR, assist pilots with readbacks, identify operational changes such as runway assignments, approaches, altitude clearances and frequencies, and synchronize flight context. Relevant communications would be available in DIARIO and FLIGHT DEBRIEF, subject to API capabilities and your approval.

ATC instructions will remain advisory within COPILOTO VECTOR. The application will not automatically transmit radio responses or control aircraft systems without appropriate pilot authorization. Integrations use replaceable adapters; generic simulator support remains available.

We are requesting a distribution developer key to support future users of our desktop application. We would be pleased to provide further technical documentation and demonstration materials for your review.
